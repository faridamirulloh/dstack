# Long-form Documents

Use available document search or user-provided files and links.

Search for the domain term, feature, symbol, project name, decision wording, and relevant dates. Look for RFCs, meeting notes, launch plans, ADRs, and post-launch reviews. Follow outbound links to the original issue, review, or data when possible.

Record title, section, author, date, and stable path or URL. Draft proposals show considered intent; only accepted decisions and subsequent implementation evidence establish what the team chose.
