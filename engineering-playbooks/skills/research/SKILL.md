# Research

Choose a mode from the requested outcome.

## Rationale mode

Use for "why" questions and stay read-only.

1. Define the claim to explain and plausible alternatives.
2. Gather repository evidence: code, tests, documentation, issue or change history when available and authorized.
3. Gather external primary sources when the claim depends on standards, product behavior, or current facts.
4. Separate observed fact, sourced claim, and inference. Reconcile conflicts instead of averaging them.
5. Answer directly with citations and confidence: high, medium, or low, plus what would change the conclusion.

Use [epistemics](references/rationale/epistemics.md) and the [source playbook](references/rationale/source-playbook.md) for deeper investigations.

## Research-artifact mode

Use only when the user asks for a saved research document.

1. Agree or infer a narrow research question and intended reader.
2. Prefer primary, current, and independently corroborated sources.
3. Write a Markdown artifact at the requested path, with a descriptive title, executive answer, evidence, counterevidence, uncertainties, citations, and date-sensitive caveats.
4. Verify that every material claim has a source or is labelled as inference.

Do not write a file in rationale mode. Do not manufacture quotes, history, or citations.
